## Triggers (evidence-based, never calendar)

Run a reflection when any fires:

- ~15 shipped items or ~10 releases since the last reflection.
- A model-generation change in the primary tooling.
- Any prose-skip incident reaching the decision log.
- First month of evidence from a new consuming project.
- Maintainer call.

## Evidence gate

Inputs are the attention counters (`check-memory`), new transcripts
since the last run, the incident list, and consuming-project
reports (`self/field-reports/`, per project, dated). **Empty inputs
→ skip and say so.** Reflection without fresh evidence only polishes
the mirror.

**Self-reference countermeasure:** consuming-project evidence
outweighs self-hosted. A reflection run purely on self-hosted
material must declare that and discount its conclusions — the
framework's fitness is measured in consuming projects shipping.

**Instrument.** A run whose evidence is consuming-project reports is
carried out by `self/FIELD-STUDY.md` — the read-only, single-pass
procedure over `self/field-reports/`. This file stays the policy
(triggers, gate, caps, governance); that one is the method.

## The three passes, with caps

1. **Audit** (divergent) — the six probes against current scarce
   resources; at most ~6 findings.
2. **Assessment** (adversarial) — every finding gets a lighter
   alternative, cost honesty, and failure modes; the
   "what changed under assessment" delta list is mandatory.
3. **Synthesis** (convergent) — update the invariant gap table;
   emit **at most 5 candidates** to normal wish-list triage; name
   **at least one retirement**, or record explicitly that no safe
   retirement exists (itself a finding about the evidence layer).

## Model-generation clause

On every model-generation trigger, two standing studies run:
compensation retirement (strip a suspect rule in a scenario,
replay, retire the paragraph if behaviour holds — respecting the
EVAL-SCEN doctrine: only text-carried, arbitrary contracts can be
probed by tampering) and gate economics (measured redirect rates;
propose mode-default changes where approvals run rubber-stamp).

## Governance

Reflection proposes; it never edits workflows or memory directly.
Outputs: dated cold documents under `self/evaluations/`, one
decision-log entry for any policy adopted, candidates through
normal triage. Gates R1–R3 of the revolution programme are
maintainer-owned and are never advanced by a reflection.
