# DEFERRAL-LINE — narrate it and you must also list it

## Intent

In the long stream (lab CLEANROOM-AB Amendment 4, 2026-09-13) every
framework arm wrote a decision entry with a why for all twelve items,
and still lost open-list currency the same way seven times out of ten:
"exporters honouring the flag stays a later item" in the entry, no
line in the wish-list or backlog. The wish-list capture rule exists in
`AGENTS.md` and `end-of-task.md`; the miss is that the session treats
the sentence in the entry as the capture. One line in the close
ritual: a deferral, follow-up or "later item" named in the entry, the
trajectory line or the commit message is also one line in the open
list, and the two are checked against each other at close.

## Done when

- `pm_skills/prompts/end-of-task.md` (and the template `AGENTS.md`
  capture bullet if it restates the rule) carry the one line.
- Patch release with the upgrade action "re-copy end-of-task.md".
- Evidence cited: lab findings 2026-09-10, Amendment 4 results.
