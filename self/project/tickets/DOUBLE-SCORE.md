# DOUBLE-SCORE — a blind second pass on judged measures

## Intent

6 of 48 cells and was right on 2 of them: the first pass had read the
decision-log, trajectory and records for a parked deferral and missed
the minimal contract's wish-list. A findings table changed. The
instrument should require the second pass wherever a dimension needs
judgement rather than counting, and say how disagreements are settled.
